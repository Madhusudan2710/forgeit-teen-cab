THE NEED

Growing up means
going places.

But getting there should not mean compromising on safety. Today's ride options were not designed around the needs of teens and the people who care for them.

↗

No dedicated cab system

Existing ride-hailing apps are built for adults. Teens are left using platforms not designed for their safety or needs.

◉

Critical safety concerns

Parents have little visibility into a teen's ride, with no dedicated real-time tracking, emergency alerts or verified driver profiles.

⌁

Zero parental supervision

Once a teen books a ride, parents can be left in the dark — without oversight, control or peace of mind.

THE JOURNEY

Simple steps.
Safer journeys.

From registration to ride — a connected experience that keeps parents involved and teens moving.

♧

Parent registers

⌘

Teen profile linked
